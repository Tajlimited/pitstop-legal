Safe House Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

What we collect

  • Location — your device location is used only to center the map near you. It is never stored on our servers and never shared with other users.
  • Content you post — locations, star ratings, review text, and the first name you sign in with are stored in Apple iCloud (CloudKit) and shown publicly to other users of the app.
  • Sign in with Apple — we receive only the name you approve sharing. We never see your email, password, or Apple ID credentials.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no trackers, no analytics SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties.
  • No background location tracking. Location is used only while the app is open.

Data storage

Public content (pins, ratings, reviews) is stored in Apple CloudKit. Your sign-in state and preferences are stored only on your device.

Deleting your data

To request removal of content you posted, contact taj@tajlimited.com.
